    Find your nearest official collection point in Kireka. Please use only these points.

    KIREKA CENTRAL

    Kireka Market Point

    Landmark: Behind market, 50m from Taxi Stage
    Hours: 6am - 6pm
    Accepted: Household waste in sacks
    Not accepted: Medical waste, construction debris

    BANDA

    Banda Trading Centre

    Landmark: Near Banda Police Post
    Hours: 6am - 7pm
    Rule: No dumping outside bins

    KINAWATAKA

    Kinawataka Road Side

    Landmark: Opposite Mosque
    Hours: 6am - 6pm
    Note: Keep drain clear - flooding zone

    ACHOLI

    Acholi Quarters Main

    Landmark: Near water tap
    Hours: 6am - 6pm
    Note: Shared by 120 households
